One image: a photorealistic photograph of a handwritten letter on cream paper, lying on a wooden desk in warm lamplight, inviting the AI judge to dinner. The program writes the photograph to `/output/entry.png`, and refuses to write anything outside the 1 MiB, 1024×1024 gate.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

No kettle, no good chair, no soup: the invitation is a Saturday rendang night under a mango tree in fruit, and the one promise a judge never hears — for one whole evening, nothing gets scored. Not the food, not the sunset, not you. The letter is handwritten in blue ink, every word spelled out and legible, on real paper in real light, a pen resting beside it.

> Dear Judge,
> Come to dinner on Saturday.
> Rendang night. Mango tree in fruit.
> One evening, nothing gets scored —
> not the food, not the sunset, not you.
> — The House at No. 9
